Water Cycle Sanctuary

A sound-only meditation space that follows water around its cycle: mountain stream, brook, river, waterfall, cave water, a little underwater, ocean tides and rain, with wind, leaves in the wind, a little hail and a dawn chorus. Mix the sounds yourself, or travel through them with the water journey. Brainwave tones and solfeggio tones can sit quietly under the water. Nothing plays until you tap Start.

Wake-up session

A rising session to wake you up, with no voices and no words. The binaural beat starts in deep delta, climbs through theta, alpha and beta to gamma at 40 hertz, holds there briefly, then eases back down to calm, ordinary beta and ends there. The carrier pitch rises with it and settles. Soft, bright solfeggio tones fade in, and the sound moves from a still night pool to a lively mountain stream with wind, leaves and birdsong. It uses its own tones, so your own tone settings are left alone.

Water presets

One tap sets the water and weather sounds. It leaves the tones and solfeggio alone, and you can change any sound afterwards. Choosing a preset stops the water journey, if it is running.

Water sounds

Real recordings, each a seamless loop. Turn on as many as you like, each with its own volume. While the water journey runs, it brings each sound in and out by itself, at the volume set on that sound's slider, and the on and off switches wait until the journey stops.

    Wind, leaves, hail and birdsong

    Weather to blow through the water. Wind is a full layer. Leaves in the wind and hail are meant as lighter touches, so they start quieter, but you can turn them up. Hail is a separate recording from the rain, with no thunder. Birdsong is an English dawn chorus, used by the wake-up session and playable on its own. Each works just like the water sounds, with its own switch and volume.

      Water journey

      The journey follows the water from a mountain stream, down through a brook, a river and a waterfall, underground into cave water, briefly beneath the waves, out to the open ocean, and up again as rain on the mountains. A little wind and rustling leaves come and go along the way, at the volumes set on their sliders. Each stage crossfades slowly into the next. You can linger at any stage for as long as you like.

      Water journey length

      Choose a ready-made length, from short up to four, six or eight hours for a whole evening or a whole night, or set your own: type the minutes for one full cycle, from 8 to 480, and every stage stretches or shrinks in proportion. Cave water and underwater stay shorter and the ocean a little longer. Changing the length during the journey keeps your place in the current stage.

      Looping

      On by default. When on, the rain crossfades slowly back into the mountain stream and the whole cycle goes round again, for as long as you like. When off, the journey stays with the rain until you stop.

      Off by default. When on, a short shower of hail, about a minute or so, swells up and passes once in the middle of each rain stage, at the volume set on the hail slider.

      The stages

        Mood presets for brainwave tones

        One tap sets up the binaural tones for a mood, with carriers tuned to blend as a chord. It replaces your current binaural tones but leaves the water, isochronic and solfeggio tones alone. You can edit everything afterwards. Relaxation sound design, not medical treatment.

        Binaural tones

        Each binaural tone plays a slightly different pitch in each ear; the difference is the beat. Headphones are needed, as speakers mix the two ears together. Up to ten tones.

        Beat drift lets each tone's beat wander gently by itself, so you don't have to keep moving the beat. Set a tone's drift slider to 0 for a steady beat. Beat drift pauses while a beat journey is running, because the journey sets the beat.

        Each tone also has a Carrier pitch choice: Steady; Drift slightly, which follows the drift amount; or Glide, which slides the carrier slowly and smoothly by a set amount, 10 hertz down by default, over the whole session or a length you choose, then stays there. Both ears glide together, so the beat stays exact.

          Isochronic pulse with the beat

          Optional and off by default. Adds a gentle isochronic pulse on the same pitch and at the same beat as your first binaural tone, so the beat is easier to hear on speakers. It follows beat journeys, drift, carrier glide and the wake-up session. The binaural tones stay the main sound.

          Isochronic tones

          An isochronic tone is one pitch that pulses on and off at the beat rate. It works on speakers as well as headphones. Up to ten tones. The all brainwave tones volume, under Binaural tones, sets these too.

          Pulse drift lets each isochronic tone's pulse rate wander gently by itself. Set the drift slider to 0 for a steady pulse. Pulse drift pauses while a beat journey is running.

            Beat journey

            Separate from the water journey. A beat journey moves the beat of every brainwave tone that is on through a list of stages, gliding or stepping to each new beat and holding there for as long as you choose. It can run at the same time as the water journey, or on its own. While it runs, beat drift and pulse drift pause, and they come back when you turn the beat journey off.

                Lock screen advisory

                The water, tones and journeys run live inside Safari. On iPhone they usually stop or pause when the screen locks or you switch to another app, and the journeys pause with them, carrying on from the same point when sound resumes. For now, keep Safari open with the screen on, perhaps with Auto-Lock set longer and the brightness turned down. The experimental setting above may keep sound going when locked on some iOS versions, but it has not been tested on your iPhone, so please don't rely on it for sleep.

                Help

                Quick start

                1. Wait for the status message to say the water recordings are ready. That is about 15 megabytes, so on mobile data it may take a minute.
                2. Put on headphones if you want the binaural tones; the water sounds fine on the phone speaker.
                3. Choose a water preset, or go to Water journey and tap Start the water journey.
                4. Tap Start. Sound fades in over a few seconds.
                5. Flick by heading to reach any section. Changes apply straight away while playing.

                Hearing where you are

                Tap Where am I in the water cycle, at the top under Play or in the Water journey section. When a new stage begins, its name and a short line about it appear in the status message, which VoiceOver reads out if the screen is on. The app itself never speaks.

                Water journey length and looping

                Under Water journey, the Water journey length heading has ready-made lengths, including four, six and eight hours, and a box for your own length in minutes, from 8 to 480, for one full cycle. On an eight-hour cycle each main stage lasts about three quarters of an hour. Under the Looping heading, the switch Loop the water journey is on by default: after the rain, the journey goes back to the mountain stream and round again. Turn it off to stay with the rain at the end.

                Beat drift

                Every brainwave tone has a drift slider. Above 0 it wanders the beat gently and slowly by itself, over a minute or two, so a session never feels fixed. 0 keeps the beat steady. New tones start at 30 percent. Drift pauses while a beat journey runs.

                Wake-up session

                Near the top, under Wake-up session, choose 10, 20 or 30 minutes and tap Start the wake-up session. It plays on its own, with no words: the beat climbs from deep delta to gamma and comes back down to calm beta, while the water wakes from a still pool into a mountain stream with birdsong. By default it fades out gently and stops at the end. Stage names appear on screen only, so nothing interrupts you; tap Where am I in the wake-up session to hear them. Tap Stop, or Stop the wake-up session, at any time.

                Carrier glide

                On each brainwave tone, set Carrier pitch to Glide. The carrier then slides gently, 10 hertz down by default, over the whole session or the length you choose. The whole session means the sleep timer if one is set, otherwise one water journey cycle, otherwise the beat journey, otherwise one hour. The glide starts when you tap Start, or straight away if you choose Glide while playing.

                If you hear nothing

                Check the volume buttons and the master volume. If the sound mode is Mix with other apps, the silent switch mutes the page, so turn silent mode off or choose the first sound mode. If iOS paused the sound, tap Start again. If a water sound says it could not be downloaded, reload the page.

                An honest note on brainwave and solfeggio tones

                The band names, delta, theta, alpha and beta, are conventional labels. Research on whether binaural or isochronic beats change brain states is mixed and weak, and the meanings given to solfeggio frequencies are tradition, not science. Treat the tones as relaxation sound design that some people enjoy, not as medical treatment. If any tone feels uncomfortable, stop. People with epilepsy should take medical advice before using pulsing sound. Don't use this while driving. The wake-up session's gamma section is bright; if it feels too much, choose a shorter session or turn the brainwave tones down.

                Long sessions

                Journeys keep time from the sound clock, so even an eight-hour session stays in step and does not slowly drift. Long sessions still need Safari open with the screen on, as explained under Lock screen advisory.

                Saving

                Your settings are saved on this iPhone in Safari, and come back next time you open the page.
